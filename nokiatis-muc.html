Nokiatis MUC — free Android music player

Nokiatis MUC (also written Nokiatis or نوكياتس MUC) is a free music player for Android made by Team Nokiatis. It plays your music with word-by-word synced lyrics, offline downloads, smart mixes built on the phone itself, home-screen widgets and true background playback that keeps going with the screen off.

Download the latest APK

Latest version: 1.20.2 · Android 7.0+ · ≈ 47.9 MB · free

What you get

  • Synced lyrics — word-by-word karaoke timing with a live lyric strip under the cover.
  • Offline downloads — keep songs on the phone and play them with no internet.
  • Smart mixes — Daylist, Release Radar, Made For You and stations, computed on the device.
  • Widgets — four home-screen layouts, from a big artwork tile to a compact bar.
  • Real background playback — lock-screen controls, headset and Bluetooth handling.
  • Free, no ads — no advertising SDK, no analytics, no account required.

How to install Nokiatis MUC

  1. Download the APK (about 48 MB).
  2. Open the downloaded file — Android opens its installer.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser or Files app to install apps once.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.

Updates install over the previous version with the same signature, so your library, playlists, downloads and history stay in place.

Frequently asked questions

What is Nokiatis MUC?
A free Android music player by Team Nokiatis with synced lyrics, offline downloads, widgets and background playback.
Is it free?
Yes — free to download and use, with no subscription. An account is optional.
Is it safe?
Every release is signed with the same official Team Nokiatis certificate and published with its SHA-256 hash; the page can verify the file in your browser. Android warns about any app installed outside Google Play — that warning is normal.
Which Android versions work?
Android 7.0 (Nougat) and newer, including Samsung Galaxy phones.
Do updates remove my music?
No. The app data — library, playlists, downloads, history, settings — is kept by Android when the update installs over the old version.
Do I need an account?
No. Nokiatis Cloud is optional and only used to sync your library to another phone.
Is there an iPhone version?
Not at the moment; Nokiatis MUC is Android-only.
